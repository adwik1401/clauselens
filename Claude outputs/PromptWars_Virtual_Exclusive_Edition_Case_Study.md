## Problem Statement

### AI for Legal Assistance & Access

**Challenge Theme:** Legal Information and Assistance

Legal information can often be complex, difficult to understand, and challenging to navigate without professional guidance. Your task is to build an AI-powered solution that helps users navigate, understand, and work with legal information more effectively.

#### Potential Use Cases Include:

1. **Simplifying complex legal documents** - Break down complex legal language into understandable terms
2. **Comparing contracts, agreements, or policies** - Help users understand differences between multiple documents
3. **Highlighting important clauses, obligations, risks, or inconsistencies** - Identify key sections and potential issues
4. **Answering questions based on provided legal documents** - Enable Q&A functionality over legal documents
5. **Helping users understand their options and potential next steps** - Guide users through available options
6. **Generating summaries, checklists, or other actionable outputs** - Create useful derivatives of legal content
7. **Helping users prepare information or questions for a legal professional** - Assist in preparation for professional consultations

#### Important Guidelines:

**Legal Boundary**
- Solutions should provide information and assistance, rather than replace professional legal advice
- Clearly communicate limitations and disclaimers

**Open Directions**
- The use cases listed above are intended as potential directions and are not exhaustive or prescriptive
- Feel free to explore adjacent or alternative approaches

**Innovation First**
- Participants are encouraged to explore the problem space, think creatively, and develop innovative approaches
- The goal is to demonstrate capability and innovation in applying Gen AI to legal assistance

To ensure the AI evaluator can properly assess your build, your submission must include the following components:

### 1. Deployed Prototype Link
**Requirement:** Live URL Required

- A working, live link to your application
- Must be publicly accessible for evaluation
- Should demonstrate the core functionality of your solution

### 2. GitHub Repository Link
**Requirement:** Public < 10 MB

- Your repository must be published and set to public access
- The total repository size must be less than 10 MB
- Should contain all source code for the project

### 3. Project Description
**Requirement:** Brief Overview

- A brief description of the project you have built
- Clearly explain the problem it solves
- Keep it concise but comprehensive

### 4. GenAI Architecture
**Requirement:** Explicit Mapping

- Clearly and explicitly mention which Gen AI services have been used
- Specify exactly where they are integrated in your solution
- Show the data flow and integration points

### 5. Project Demo Video
**Requirement:** Walkthrough Link

- A link to a walkthrough video for evaluators to review
- Must demonstrate your project in action

## Video Guide Requirements

Your demo video is how the new AI engine processes your submission. Follow these guidelines carefully:

### Video Content Requirements:

**Time Limit:** Strictly < 4 Minutes
- Keep total length strictly under 4 minutes
- Exceeding time limit may result in evaluation penalties

**Core Walkthrough**
- Provide a clear walkthrough of your core features
- Demonstrate the main functionality step-by-step
- Show how users interact with your application

**Live Testing (Critical)**
- Enter data live on screen; do not use pre-filled forms
- Demonstrate real-time functionality
- Show genuine interactions with your system

**Highlight GenAI**
- Explicitly show where GenAI is used in your solution
- Demonstrate the dynamic output generated by AI
- Make AI integration visible and clear to evaluators

**Clear & Easy Steps**
- Ensure each step is clear and easy to follow
- Use good quality video (clear audio and visuals)
- Make it easy for evaluators to understand your solution
