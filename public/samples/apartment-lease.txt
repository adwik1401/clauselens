RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

SECTION 1. TERM
1.1 This lease begins on the Commencement Date and continues for twelve (12) months, automatically renewing for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the term ends.

SECTION 2. RENT AND FEES
2.1 Tenant shall pay rent on the first of each month. A late fee of ten percent (10%) of the monthly rent applies after a two (2) day grace period.
2.2 Tenant is responsible for all repairs, including structural and HVAC repairs, regardless of cause.

SECTION 3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Landlord may retain the security deposit for any reason at Landlord's sole discretion, with no itemized accounting required.

SECTION 4. ENTRY AND INSPECTION
4.1 Landlord may enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection purposes.

SECTION 5. SUBLETTING
5.1 Tenant may not sublet the premises under any circumstances.

SECTION 6. LIABILITY WAIVER
6.1 Landlord shall not be liable for any injury or property damage on the premises, including damage caused by Landlord's negligence.

SECTION 7. GOVERNING LAW
7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
