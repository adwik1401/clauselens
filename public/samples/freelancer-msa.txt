4.1 Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from any and all claims, damages, and liabilities arising from the Services, without limitation and without any corresponding obligation on Client.

SECTION 5. TERMINATION
5.1 Client may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice. Contractor may terminate only with sixty (60) days' written notice.

SECTION 6. NON-COMPETE

7.1 Any dispute shall be resolved through binding arbitration. Contractor waives any right to participate in a class action or jury trial.
